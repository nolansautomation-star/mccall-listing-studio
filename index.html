What's in each package

Every listing package includes the copy and layouts needed to market the home, written from the details the agent provides.

MLS listing description

Long and short versions, plus headline options.

Social posts

Instagram, Facebook, and LinkedIn posts for Just Listed, Open House, Under Contract, and Just Sold.

Reel / TikTok script

A 30–45 second walkthrough script with a shot list.

Email blast

Copy for an email to the agent's list.

One-page flyer

A flyer layout with copy. A Canva-designed flyer is included with the $179 package.

Fact checklist

So every detail matches what the agent provided.
