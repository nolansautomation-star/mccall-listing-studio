02 — Process

Simple on purpose.

  1. 01

    Send listing details and photos

    Email the listing details and photos for the home.

  2. 02

    Get a complete package back

    A complete, ready-to-review marketing package: MLS copy, social posts, a Reel script, an email blast, a flyer, and a fact checklist.

  3. 03

    Review it and pay by invoice

    Check the package against the fact checklist, then use it. Billing is per listing through a Stripe invoice.

03 — Fair Housing

Describe the home.
Never the buyer.

Copy follows Fair Housing guidelines. It describes the property and never the ideal buyer.

Every package uses only facts the agent provides, and the fact checklist lets the agent confirm each one.
